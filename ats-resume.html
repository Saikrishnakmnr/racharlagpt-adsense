Building an ATS-friendly resume from real facts

An ATS-friendly resume is primarily a readable, truthful document with clear sections and job-relevant evidence.

Use a predictable structure

Use clear headings such as Professional Summary, Technical Skills, Education, Work Experience, Projects and Certifications when they apply.

Match the role truthfully

Identify responsibilities, tools and skills you genuinely have. Emphasize relevant experience you actually performed. Never add unsupported technologies or achievements.

Prefer evidence over buzzwords

Describe the project or responsibility that demonstrates a skill. Where your real experience supports it, include measurable outcomes.

Keep formatting simple

Use standard text, consistent headings and readable bullets. Avoid putting essential information only inside images or decorative elements.

Review factual accuracy

Check names, dates, employers, education, projects, links and contact details. The candidate remains responsible for the truth of the final resume.

Tailoring checklist

  1. Identify the target role.
  2. Extract relevant requirements.
  3. Map requirements to real evidence.
  4. Rewrite bullets for clarity.
  5. Remove unsupported claims.
  6. Proofread the final document.
