About RacharlaGPT

RacharlaGPT is an independent AI learning and productivity application created and developed by Racharla Saikrishna.

Purpose

The project is intended to make practical AI assistance available for learning, coding, data analysis, project work, resume preparation and career practice.

How the service works

The interactive application uses Streamlit for its interface, Supabase for account/session and saved-chat services, and Groq for AI processing and voice transcription. The public website is separate and provides information and learning resources.

Independent service

RacharlaGPT is not an official Google, Groq, Supabase or OpenAI product. References to third-party services describe technologies used by the application.

AI limitations

AI output can be incomplete or wrong. Users should review important results and use appropriate primary or professional sources for high-stakes decisions.
